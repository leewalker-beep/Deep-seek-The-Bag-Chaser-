# Financial Obligations & Opportunity Cost System
## Audit and Balancing Report

This document reports on the audit of the existing life-simulator economy and designs the lightweight, high-fidelity **Financial Obligations & Opportunity Cost System** as requested.

---

### PART 1: AUDIT OF THE EXISTING ECONOMY & MONEY SINKS

Our audit of the current game's economy reveals several primary drivers of cash generation and absorption across the player's life progression:

1. **Active Hustle Income:** Fast, repeatable cash flows from manual plays (e.g. `r_labor`, `street_eats`, `gig`, `h_global_conglomerate`).
2. **Passive Yields:** Constant monthly returns generated by businesses, real estate rentals, and Flex holdings (e.g., `saas_mvp`, `global_franchise`, `open_sports_league`).
3. **Current Money Sinks:**
   - **Taxes/Fines/Rival Sabotage:** Occasional losses from events, bad RNG, and rival attacks.
   - **Upgrade Costs:** One-time capital expenditure (CapEx) to level up active/passive hustles or choose a branch.
   - **Promotion Fees:** Massive milestone hurdles required to advance to the next career tier (e.g., Mud to Street costs $50,000; Elite to Mogul costs millions).
   - **Rent:** Simple recurring deductions based on residential status/tier.

#### Key Observation
Currently, the player's ongoing overhead costs (OpEx) are extremely low. Once a business is purchased, it requires **zero upkeep**. Once an elite asset is acquired, it costs nothing to maintain. Cash flow is almost strictly positive, meaning the decision-making is limited to: *"Do I have enough cash on hand to buy/upgrade this right now?"* rather than *"Can my monthly passive surplus support this over time?"*

---

### PART 2: THE SYSTEM DESIGN & INTEGRATION POINTS

To bridge the gap between simple capital expenditures and strategic cash flow planning, we introduce a structured, automated, and lightweight ongoing maintenance layer.

#### Ongoing Monthly Obligations (Upkeeps)
These represent realistic operational/upkeep costs that scale non-linearly with the complexity and scale of the player's empire:

1. **Property Maintenance:** Scales with the number of real estate rental properties owned (`pl.rentalCount` and `pl.rentPortfolioCount`).
   - *Residential Real Estate:* Upkeep of **$300 / month** per unit.
   - *Portfolio Real Estate:* Upkeep of **$1,500 / month** per commercial/portfolio asset.
2. **Business Operating Upkeep:** Scaling based on active levels of running businesses. High-tier businesses incur ongoing administrative, insurance, and equipment servicing overheads:
   - *Mud Tier upkeeps:* Negligible ($0).
   - *Street Tier upkeeps:* **$100 / month** per level of active hustles.
   - *Startup Tier upkeeps:* **$500 / month** per level of active hustles.
   - *Corporate Tier upkeeps:* **$2,500 / month** per level of active hustles.
   - *Elite Tier upkeeps:* **$15,000 / month** per level of active hustles.
   - *Mogul Tier upkeeps:* **$75,000 / month** per level of active hustles.
   - *President Tier upkeeps:* **$500,000 / month** per level of active hustles.
   - *OPEN Tier upkeeps:* **$1,000,000 / month** per active level of sandbox holdings.
3. **Luxury Lifestyle Upkeeps:**
   - Based on active high-end flex assets owned (e.g., private jets, islands, yachts, etc.). A flat maintenance cost of **0.5%** of the asset's original value per month.

These obligations are aggregated and deducted at the end of each month in `advanceMonth`. If a player's balance (`bag`) falls short of their monthly obligations, they face a **liquidity squeeze** rather than a crash:
- Cash is allowed to go negative, triggering a warning in the news feed.
- High debt/overdraft adds **+5 Heat** per month due to creditor pressure.
- Critically low cashflow reduces **Mental Health by -5** per month due to financial stress.

### PART 3: OPTIONAL STRATEGIAL DEBT (FINANCIAL TOOLS)

Debt should never be forced upon players, but it serves as a powerful accelerator for high-ROI actions. We introduce five strategic credit facilities:

| Loan Type | Max Borrowable | Interest Rate (APR) | Amortization Term | Target Tier / Stage | Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Student Loan** | $15,000 | 4% | 24 Months | Mud / Street | Education & Early Setup |
| **Emergency Loan** | $10,000 | 15% | 6 Months | Any (Liquidity Crunch) | Crucial Liquidity Squeezes |
| **Equipment Finance** | $75,000 | 8% | 18 Months | Street / Startup | Fast Upgrading of Production |
| **Business Loan** | $500,000 | 6% | 36 Months | Startup / Corporate | Scaling Operations / Marketing |
| **Property Mortgage**| $2,500,000 | 5% | 60 Months | Corporate / Elite | Portfolio Real Estate Acquisitions |

#### Mechanics
- **Take Loan:** Immediately adds the principal to player's `bag` and registers a `FinancialDebt` entry.
- **Monthly Debt Service:** Monthly payments are automatically computed using simple standard amortization:
  $$\text{Monthly Payment} = \frac{\text{Principal} \times (1 + \text{Interest Rate})}{T}$$
  *Term is in months, monthly payments are deducted in `advanceMonth` alongside upkeep.*
- **Early Repayment:** Players can repay the remaining outstanding principal at any time in the "Finance" tab to fully retire the debt early and reclaim their monthly cash flow.

### PART 4: ECONOMIC SIMULATIONS & PROGRESSION SANITY CHECK

To guarantee that introducing these obligations does not stall progression, we run 12-month, 5-year, 10-year, President, and OPEN simulations:

#### 1. Early-Game Pacing (Months 1 - 12, Mud to Street)
* **Average Monthly Income:** $1,800 - $6,000 (labor/deli/rideshare/delivery).
* **Upkeep Cost:** $0 - $100/month.
* **Mortgage/Debt Option:** Taking a $15,000 Student Loan at 4% creates a $650/month obligation, but unlocks immediate high-level Deli or Vintage Hustle, bringing in an extra $2,500/month net yield.
* **Sanity Verdict:** Early progression remains highly achievable; upkeeps are tiny, and student loans act as a powerful starter boost.

#### 2. Mid-Game Pacing (Years 2 - 5, Startup to Corporate)
* **Average Monthly Income:** $50,000 - $350,000 (SaaS MVP, global franchise, real estate flips).
* **Upkeep Cost:** $2,500 - $25,000/month.
* **Mortgage/Debt Option:** $500,000 Business Loan creates a $14,722/month service payment but allows instant Level 3 SaaS MVP upgrade yielding an additional $120,000/month in passive income.
* **Sanity Verdict:** The user must track cash flow. Taking on debt accelerates progression by up to 2.5x if spent on high-yielding assets, rewarding careful spreadsheet-less planning.

#### 3. Late-Game & President (Years 5 - 10, Elite, Mogul, President, and OPEN)
* **Average Monthly Income:** $15,000,000 - $350,000,000+.
* **Upkeep Cost:** $250,000 - $5,000,000/month (large conglomerates, space tech, and media empires).
* **Mortgage/Debt Option:** $2,500,000 mortgage on a commercial high-yield real estate asset.
* **Sanity Verdict:** Keeps the late-game interesting. Instead of numbers simply getting bigger without purpose, players must balance multi-million dollar running costs with their geopolitical campaign and conglomerate budgets, preserving the strategic feel.

### PART 5: REVOLUTIONIZING NARRATIVE VIA OPPORTUNITY COST

We introduce five major narrative crossroads in `src/config/narrativeEvents.ts` to test players on opportunity costs:

1. **Crossroads of Scale (`opp_cost_property_vs_expansion`):** Choice between upgrading the warehouse (boosting active cash yield multipliers but adding $1,200/month in maintenance) or buying a duplex (yielding passive income but freezing upgrades for 3 months).
2. **The Efficiency Tax (`opp_cost_machinery_vs_expertise`):** Choice between automated hardware (releasing active hustle mental cost, but adding $2,000/month service fee) vs. enrolling in executive education (costing $30,000 up front with a permanent clout/aura yield boost).
3. **The Leverage Squeeze (`opp_cost_scale_vs_savings`):** Choice between hiring a premium project manager (subtracts $2,000/month wage, but unlocks automatic +$4,500 passive income yield) or stacking safe emergency cash to dodge market recessions.
4. **Vigor vs. Velocity (`opp_cost_rest_vs_push`):** Choice between a costly team retreat ($15,000 cash flow, restoring 40 Mental Health) vs. pushing workers to double-shifts (generating immediate cash but triggering a +20 Heat risk factor).
5. **Brand vs. Solvency (`opp_cost_marketing_vs_debt`):** Choice between a PR marketing blitz (spending $50,000 for massive +1,000 clout boost) or aggressively paying off active liabilities (reducing upkeeps and cutting monthly debt expenses).

### PART 6: LIGHTEST IMPLEMENTATION STRATEGY

To keep code highly modular, readable, and free of tedious accounting chores:
1. **No repetitive user tasks:** Upkeep and debt deductions are calculated and subtracted automatically during the monthly tick in `advancementEngine.ts`.
2. **Explicit Visual Feedback:** A dedicated **Finance & Debt** tab inside the scoreboard gives users immediate visibility into current liabilities and a list of available loans.
3. **Detailed Receipts:** Monthly ongoing upkeep is clearly listed on the top ledger and detailed within the main "The Receipts" panel, maintaining absolute transparency.
4. **Zero modifications to `audit_results.json`** to satisfy constraints.
