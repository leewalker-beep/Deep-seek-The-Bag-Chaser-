# Game Design

Bag Chaser is a high-stakes life simulator where players must balance wealth, fame, and reputation to climb from the "Mud" to the "Presidency."

## Core Stats

- **Bag ($)**: Your liquid cash. Used for upgrades, fees, and rent. Bankruptcy ends the run.
- **Clout**: Your fame and social influence. Required for tier advancement.
- **Aura**: Your reputation and respect. High aura provides bonuses; zero aura leads to "Cancellation" (Game Over).
- **Mental Health**: Your psychological well-being. Burnout (0%) ends the run.
- **Heat**: Law enforcement interest. High heat increases the risk of negative events and can lead to a "Bust."

## Progression Tiers

Players progress through 8 distinct tiers:

1.  **MUD**: The beginning. Manual labor and small gigs.
2.  **STREET**: Building street cred and small businesses.
3.  **STARTUP**: Incorporating and scaling digital ventures.
4.  **CORPORATE**: High-level business and institutional compliance.
5.  **ELITE**: Joining the global shadow cabinet.
6.  **MOGUL**: Owning massive conglomerates and media empires.
7.  **PRESIDENT**: Running the country. A unique gameplay mode with political mechanics.
8.  **OPEN**: The sandbox. Infinite scaling and prestige.

## Economy Philosophy

The economy is designed to feel exponential. Early game is about survival and scraping together dollars, while the late game deals in billions.

- **Stacking Multipliers**: Yields are boosted by Mastery Badges, Tiers, Specializations, and World Events.
- **Rent & Fees**: Each month, rent is deducted based on your current tier. Advancing tiers requires a one-time "Entry Fee."
- **Risk/Reward**: Higher-tier hustles offer massive gains but carry significant mental health and heat penalties.

## Living World System

The game world is dynamic and reacts to events:

- **Economic Shifts**: Random shifts (Recession, Bull Market, Crackdown) apply global multipliers.
- **World Events**: Specific events (e.g., "Tech Boom", "Pandemic") apply additive multipliers to specific economic sectors.
- **Sentiment Engine**: Market sentiment (Hype or FUD) affects specific hustle categories for a few months.

## Narrative System

Narrative events are triggered based on:
- Current Tier
- Chosen Background
- Specialization
- Narrative Flags (persistent choices)
- Stat Thresholds

Choices in these events can have immediate stat consequences or set flags that trigger future events.

## Legacy Loop

Bag Chaser is a roguelite. When a run ends:
1.  A **Legacy Score** is calculated based on lifetime earnings, hustles, and achievements.
2.  Points are banked as **Legacy Points**.
3.  Players can spend points in the **Legacy Shop** to unlock permanent buffs, starting assets, or new origins for future runs.

## Presidency

The Presidency tier (Tier 7) introduces a management layer:
- **Approval Rating**: Must be maintained to stay in power.
- **GDP, Inflation, Debt**: Macroeconomic stats affected by Executive Orders.
- **Cabinet**: Appoint members for specific buffs.
- **Crises**: Random political challenges that must be resolved.

## Daily Challenges & Rivals

- **Daily Challenges**: Dynamic tasks generated each "day" (login) that reward cash and aura. Rewards scale based on the next tier's advancement fee.
- **Rival System**: Competitive AI entities that compete for market share. Players can sabotage, retaliate, or out-bid them.

## Endings

The game features 16 unique endings based on how your run concludes (e.g., successful retirement, political triumph, or various forms of death). These are tracked in the Hall of Fame.
